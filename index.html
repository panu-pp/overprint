Drag pieces onto the 8×8 sheet. Full rows and columns clear. A line in one ink is a pure line and scores double.
Cleared blocks fill the ink meters. 6 blocks = 1 charge.
Every line clear starts a CHAIN: the clear plays, your perks fire one by one by themselves. Each perk that fires adds ×1 to the chain multiplier. Empty the whole board for a CLEAN SHEET: chain ×2. Clear at least once per tray to keep your STREAK: it multiplies every chain payout. Between moves, tap a perk to spend charges and arm it: its next fire is boosted.
Level ups offer 3 perks. One is always a curse: it messes with the board but adds +50% score (curses add up) and a special reward.
